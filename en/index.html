About CHHO

Integrity, quality and service for every critical infrastructure upgrade

Chieh Ho Technology was founded by professionals in IT, software development, system integration, network communications and electromechanical engineering.

We provide planning, implementation, application and integration services for energy-efficient data centers as well as wired and wireless network environments.

From Build To Operation

From data center construction to ongoing operations, we help critical systems stay reliable

Chieh Ho connects planning, construction, integration, acceptance testing and maintenance into one complete service flow, helping enterprises reduce infrastructure risk and improve long-term availability.
